## Position and boundary

This subdomain should become a technical buyer’s guide to glass toilet cubicles: material selection, safety design inputs, privacy treatments, layout, hardware, support systems, survey coordination, fabrication, installation, maintenance, and procurement.

It does not own the full multi-material cubicle niche, general architectural glazing, shower enclosures, structural glass calculations, code interpretation, or city-swapped sales content. Final glass make-up, thickness, dimensions, holes/notches, support, impact performance, accessibility, and installation require project-specific design and current standards.

## Evidence gates

- Glass type, thickness, maximum size, hole/notch limits, edgework, heat treatment, interlayer, impact behaviour, and breakage pattern require current standards, fabricator data, and project-specific competent design.
- Hardware load, cycle, corrosion, fire, accessibility, and warranty claims require named manufacturer documentation and the applicable test/classification.
- “Stainless steel,” “tempered,” “laminated,” “water resistant,” “safe,” or “tested” must include grade/make-up, test basis, and traceable certificates where relevant.
- Privacy, visibility, colour, opacity, and finish acceptance require approved physical samples/mockups under representative lighting; marketing images are not proof.
- Building and marine use cases require the owner brief, current authority rules, environmental exposure, cleaning regime, and responsible designer’s approval.
- Prices, quantities, lead times, installation productivity, service life, and guarantees require dated, like-for-like project evidence.
- Site photos, defect diagnosis, breakage accounts, and project references require provenance, permission, and context.
- If glass is chipped, cracked, incorrectly drilled, unsupported, or moving, content must recommend isolation and competent inspection rather than DIY alteration.
